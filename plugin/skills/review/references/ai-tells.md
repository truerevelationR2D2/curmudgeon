# AI tells: the default look and copy

Merged from Anthropic's frontend-design guidance and Impeccable's detector (ideas, not text). Scripted ones carry the detector rule in brackets; the rest are judged by eye from the screenshots.

**The rule that keeps false positives down:** if the brief or a recorded decision asked for the look, it is not a finding. Cite the line that asked for it.

## The five default looks
1. Cream background with a serif display face and a terracotta accent [cream-and-terracotta]
2. Near-black background with one acid-green or vermilion accent
3. Broadsheet: hairline rules, zero radius, dense newspaper columns
4. The SaaS-card kit: content chopped into identical rounded cards, one radius everywhere, the same soft shadow under each, gradient washes [one border-radius; same shadow; stock palette]
5. Template chrome: a tracked ALL-CAPS eyebrow over every heading, "A · B · C" meta strings, "WORD — fragment" labels, #111 standing in for black, monospace for small data, "→" on links and buttons [eyebrow; wide tracking; arrow]

## Type
Overused default faces (Inter, Roboto, Geist, Space Grotesk, Plus Jakarta Sans, Fraunces) [overused typeface] · one word in a headline accented · all-caps labels and body · needless labels above content · a flat scale · text under 12 px [tiny text] · tight leading under 1.3 · lines over about 80 characters · numbered markers (01/02/03) on content that isn't a sequence · gradient text [gradient text].

## Colour and surface
Indigo/violet gradients and cyan-on-dark [stock palette] · grey text on coloured backgrounds · cards inside cards · glows, halos and radial spotlights · raw hex values with no tokens [raw hex].

## Motion
Fade-and-slide-up on every section · hover lift on every card · bounce or elastic easing [bounce easing] · pulsing dots and anything infinite [infinite animation] · motion with no reduced-motion option.

## Copy
"Welcome to…" heroes · "Click here" · lorem ipsum, John Doe, Acme, example.com [placeholder] · marketing buzzwords · aphoristic one-liners ("X. No Y.") · comments that narrate the obvious [narrating comment] · success toasts with exclamation marks.
