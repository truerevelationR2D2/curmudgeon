# Checkout requirements

- **REQ-1**: The cart total is the sum of the item prices.
- **REQ-2**: A discount code reduces the total by its percentage, never below zero.
- **REQ-3**: Prices are shown with two decimal places.

| Id | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| REQ-4 | An empty cart cannot be checked out. |

Background: REQ-9 was dropped in the 2025 rewrite (mentioned here, not defined).
